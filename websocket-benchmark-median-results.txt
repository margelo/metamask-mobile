  "what does a typical message actually experience?"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  EXCLUDED (RN bridge was faster or tied at the median)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Per request, the data for these is not shown — Nitro did NOT win at p50:
    - Cold-start latency        (RN faster at p50)
    - Heartbeat PING/PONG RTT   (tied/RN faster at p50; Nitro only wins on tail)
    - Reconnect recovery        (RN faster at p50)
    - Batch subscription        (RN faster at p50)

  Takeaway: Nitro's clear median wins are in BULK / high-frequency message
  processing. For one-off connect/subscribe operations the lightweight bridge
  path is as fast or faster at the median on this simulator run.
